	>>> D3D Problems?

 - I select my 3D card in the video board list and its still fucking SLOW!

-> Did you set 'D3D rendering' option? Beware that software rendering is also
   available on 3d cards...

 - The sound is jerky...

-> Grab the latest drivers for your sound card...
   I did encounter this bug when running D3D on a S3Virge/Dx with Dsound
   AWE64 Gold and that's for sure a drivers problem.

 - The demo randomly freeze or exit without any error stuff... Why?!

-> I DON'T KNOW!!! The only thing I know is that Asus V3400 AGP16mo do
   that... I've tried to figure out WHY but didn't succeed ):... too
   bad if your card does the same!

 - When running Direct3D colors are wrong.
or When running Direct3D my monitor goes sleep mode.
or Reboot as soon as i launch it ):

-> Make sure you installed your board's manufacturer drivers...
   Sometimes drivers given with DXsetup are not reliable and may
   report bad infos about device. Also try REFERENCE drivers from
   your board's chipset manufacturer. (i.e: for an EON lilith Banshee
   download the 3dfx banshee reference drivers)

 - The render is shity!!! This suxx!

-> Errr... try software mode first as you'll always get all rendering
   types needed in 3d scenes. If it differs from what you get in D3D
   then sorry but your board (or d3d drivers) doesn't support some
   rendering types. (note that i might also have done some setup the
    wrong way! (: ). However my 3dfx Voodoo1 runs everything perfectly
   and this is something some people don't seem to understand. I just
   can't stand seeing articles saying that 3dfx is crap because of its
   16bpp output while the fucking brand new TNT can't manage mapping
   additif alpha whereas an old 3dfx 1 did it!!! Go back looking your
   amazing flat cube running 32bpp windowed... (:

 - I can't uncheck the 'fullscreen' box when i select my ...blahblah...
   board... why!?

-> Your ...blahblah... board probably doesn't support windowed D3D.
   (Voodoo chipset (not rush/banshee) for example)

 - Awfuly slow...

-> This might be because your board doesn't have enough memory to store
   all scene's texture and D3D's texture management have to swap textures
   all the time... Try the 'D3D Texture fit board's memory' option.
